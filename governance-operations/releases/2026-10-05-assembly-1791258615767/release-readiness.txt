Operational behavior that remains intentional
Registration persists while the NFT is deposited; stake/power is re-snapshotted
for every cycle. One deposited NFT per wallet; multiple wallet NFTs add no votes.
Withdrawals after a snapshot do not revoke that snapshot's eligibility. This is
pseudonymous economic resistance, not proof of one-human-one-vote.
Nonprofit onboarding remains controlled by the existing multisig/Timelock; there
is no arbitrary ballot cap. During batched preparation, processed recipients are
frozen while later recipients use the configuration when their batch is processed.
Avoid changing nonprofit configuration during preparation unless intended.
Nonzero registered NFT supply is not a participation quorum: zero turnout still
follows the existing Offering burn rule. Accidentally transferred NFTs are handled
through administrator-assisted recovery, not a public app recovery action.
