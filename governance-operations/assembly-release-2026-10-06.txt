The scheduled batch upgrades the existing governance proxy and atomically
initializes its NFT voting configuration, then binds Assembly to that proxy.
It does not start an annual voting cycle. Execution requires a fresh readiness
and dependency check; the recorded time alone is not evidence of execution.

Design: the existing OliveNFT collection remains in place. OliveAssembly is a
Timelock-owned UUPS proxy with one registered NFT per wallet and historical
registration checkpoints. Registration persists across cycles; withdrawals
remain available. Eligibility and stake-derived square-root voting power use
the cycle snapshot, with a minimum aggregate stake of 1 OBN. Both voting phases
last 30 days. There is no fixed nonprofit ballot cap; ballot preparation is
batched. Multiple NFTs in one wallet do not multiply voting power.
